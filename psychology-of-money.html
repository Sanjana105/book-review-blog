The Psychology of Money

Book Review | Morgan Housel

📖 About the Book

The Psychology of Money is a personal finance book written by Morgan Housel. It explores the way people think and behave when dealing with money.

The book explains that financial decisions are not based only on mathematics or knowledge. Personal experiences, emotions, habits and beliefs can strongly influence how people use money.

💰 Main Ideas

The book contains short stories that explore different aspects of wealth, saving, investing and financial behaviour. One of its important ideas is that being good with money is partly about behaviour.

Two people can have similar financial knowledge but make completely different financial decisions because their experiences and attitudes are different.

📌 Important Concepts

  • Saving money
  • Long-term thinking
  • Wealth and income
  • Risk and uncertainty
  • Compounding
  • Financial independence
  • Personal behaviour
  • The importance of having enough

⭐ My Review

I liked this book because it does not treat money simply as numbers. It explains the human side of financial decisions. The examples and stories made the concepts easier to understand.

One lesson I found useful is that financial success is not only about earning more money. The way we save, spend and make decisions also matters.

📚 What I Learned

This book taught me that patience is important when dealing with money. It also made me think about the difference between being wealthy and simply appearing wealthy.

📝 Conclusion

The Psychology of Money is a useful book for beginners who want to understand personal finance from a behavioural perspective. It encourages readers to think carefully about their financial habits and decisions.

My Rating

★★★★☆
