Rich Dad Poor Dad

Book Review | Robert T. Kiyosaki

📖 About the Book

Rich Dad Poor Dad is a personal-finance book written by Robert T. Kiyosaki. The book presents different ideas about money, work and financial education through the experiences of two father figures.

💰 Main Ideas

The book contrasts different attitudes toward earning, saving, investing and building wealth. One of its important concepts is the difference between assets and liabilities.

The book encourages readers to understand financial education, income generation and investing. It also suggests that people should develop financial knowledge instead of depending only on traditional education.

📌 Important Lessons

  • Financial education is important.
  • Understand the difference between assets and liabilities.
  • Learn how money is earned and managed.
  • Develop good saving and investing habits.
  • Think carefully about financial decisions.
  • Understand different sources of income.

⭐ My Review

I found Rich Dad Poor Dad interesting because it presents financial concepts through stories rather than only through technical explanations.

The book made me think about how people define financial success. It also introduced me to the importance of understanding where money comes from, where it goes and how financial decisions can affect the future.

Some of the author's claims and financial recommendations are presented from his personal perspective, so I would consider this book an introduction to financial thinking rather than a complete financial guide.

📝 Conclusion

Rich Dad Poor Dad is an interesting starting point for readers who want to learn basic concepts related to money and financial literacy. It encourages readers to become more aware of their financial decisions.

My Rating

★★★★☆
